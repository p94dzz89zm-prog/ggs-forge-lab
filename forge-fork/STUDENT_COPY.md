# Student's copiable body forecast

Natural-game review identified Student receiving a much larger damage forecast
than its actual copied body. A smaller actual-payment reproduction isolated one
cause: Giant Growth made an opposing Grizzly Bears5/5. The old offensive
ninjutsu overlay treated that live5 power as copied, forecast lethal against4
life, spent two mana and returned a3-power Dauthi Voidwalker. Actual Student
resolution copied a2/2 and dealt2. The pump is not a copiable characteristic.

The narrow correction projects the selected template through the engine's
`CardCopyService.copyStats(template, ai, false)` before scoring incoming damage
and body value. It does not change copy rules, actual clone choice, timing,
payment, deck, coefficient weights, commander protection or lethal guards.
The builder pins the previous experimental executable and verifies all JAR
entries outside the policy and its Plan record are byte-identical.

`StudentCopyRegression` checks automatic choices and actual mana, stack, return
cost, copy resolution and damage. A positive unmodified clone line must remain
executable; rejecting every Student activation is not an acceptable repair.
The forecast must not insert a live permanent. Development repeats across
three profiles are correlated constructed checks, not independent games.
Separate `--heldout` constructed conditions are evaluation-only; their outcomes
must not tune the correction or feed learning.

Limitations: the heuristic still chooses its projected template independently
of stock clone resolution. Controller-dependent continuous effects, copy-time
keywords, CDAs and trigger utility need separate modeling and regressions.
This is not a complete clone forecast or a broad pilot-strength claim. Keep the
adopted incumbent and historical engine hashes until sufficient wider reviewed
evidence justifies promotion.
