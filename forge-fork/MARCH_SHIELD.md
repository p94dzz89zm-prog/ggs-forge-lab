# Keep trample blockers during urgent March defense

A fresh cycle8 development loss exposed an actual bad target set: at5 life,
the pilot paid for March X=4 plus ward2 to phase Ezio and all three blockers
of a7/8 shrouded trampling double-striker. The unblocked trample damage killed
the pilot in first strike at-2. A separate constructed reproduction uses the
same public creature/equipment/counter mechanics and actual ward payment.
Phasing only Ezio, while keeping the blockers, survives both batches at1 life.
This demonstrates a decision error in that combat, not a certified alternate
winner for the original whole game.

During urgent empty-stack defensive combat, March now excludes our selected
blocker when it is absorbing a trampler aimed at us and that trampler is not
also selected for phasing. X is reduced to the actual remaining target count;
ordinary legality, payment and ward resolution still apply. The policy does
not manufacture a target through shroud/hexproof, alter trample, preserve dead
blockers, or assume phasing makes an already blocked attacker unblocked.

This is a conservative shield rule, not a general combat response search.
It prioritizes retaining damage absorption during serious life danger and
can forgo saving a valuable creature. Stack-threat protection, nonurgent
combat, prevention/replacement effects, first-strike phase history and complex
multiple-combat lines need broader review. Existing blockers-step forecast
limits remain; this guard does not claim a complete double-strike predictor.

MarchShieldRegression executes the real priority controller, targeted spell,
ward payment, phasing, first-strike damage, state-based deaths and regular
damage. Nine profile repetitions cover three life boundaries; two additional
controls cover no trample and ample life. These are constructed cases, not
independent natural seeds. Old failures and losing natural evidence are kept.

Four separate frozen constructed comparisons replace Bayek/Sphere Grid with
Carnage Tyrant equipped with Fireshrieker and vary life and blocker packages.
The old build dies in all four; the corrected build survives in all four with
actual priority selection, ward payment, phasing and both damage steps. This
is one deliberately selected mechanism family, not four independent natural
games or evidence of a generally stronger pilot.
