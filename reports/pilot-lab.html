What the score means

The fixed proxy combines GGS triggers per own turn (55%), opponent-player combat damage during own turns (25%), life gained per own turn (10%), and winning (10%). Effects are scaled and capped. A trigger on the stack does not guarantee a Dragon token.

It does not yet measure card advantage, every defensive benefit, missed opportunities, or whether a specific card caused the result. This is a strategy-profile learner, not a neural pilot or a system that learns card rules.
